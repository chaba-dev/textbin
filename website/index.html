How Textbin works

From clipboard to context
in four clear steps.

Keep the path from finding a problem to sharing the answer short. Textbin stays out of the way until your team needs history, policy, or proof.

  1. [+] 01.

    Paste

    Drop in code, logs, notes, or binary files. Get a clean link in one motion.

  2. ↗ 02.

    Share

    Choose the workspace, the audience, and when the paste should disappear.

  3. ⌕ 03.

    Find

    Search across the workspaces you belong to without hunting through chat.

  4. ◇ 04.

    Govern

    Make ownership, access, expiry, recovery, and audit history visible.

Built for teams

Share with confidence.

Control who can see each paste. Keep sensitive working text inside the workspace, open a deliberate link when needed, and let expiry clean up the rest.
